## Key Ideas

Predictive maintenance uses sensor data and machine learning to detect equipment problems before serious failures happen.

Data-driven AI can provide strong predictions, but it also has limitations such as limited labeled failure data, poor generalization across different operating conditions, and black-box decisions.

Neuro-symbolic AI combines neural learning with symbolic knowledge such as engineering rules, logic, and physical constraints.

One example discussed in the paper is the **Thermodynamic-Law-Integrated Autoencoder (TLI-Autoencoder)**. By adding thermodynamic knowledge during training, the reported experiment reduced the false positive rate by **77.4%** and increased the fault detection rate by **14%** compared with a standard autoencoder.

The paper also discusses Agentic AI as a possible future direction, where maintenance systems may support not only prediction but also diagnosis, planning, scheduling, and coordination.
